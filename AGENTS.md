# Product copy

When creating or changing user-facing copy, use the project-local `humanizer` skill as an editorial pass. Keep facts, user intent and necessary safety or legal context intact. Prefer specific information, status, next steps and useful labels over slogans or explanations of what the interface does. In product demos, do not repeat labels such as “demo”, “sample data” or “illustrative” on every screen; retain a clear disclosure where it matters and avoid suggesting that simulated actions are real. Remove filler rather than replacing it with different filler.
